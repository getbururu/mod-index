# Changelog

Each version has a section, newest first. A release shows its section as its notes.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Reads Star Citizen's `Game.log`: the pilot seat, quantum travel, jumps, hangars, injuries, deaths, the law, contracts, blueprints and shops. Lines about other players and other ships play nothing.
- 31 feels, each with its own level, and choices for the turn, quantum, jump, downed and fire feels.
- R2 and L2 resist in the pilot seat and on foot with something in your hand, and are free in armistice zones, while downed and in the main menu.
- The lightbar shows your health or the star system; the player LEDs count the jump drive's steps or your place in a hangar queue; the mic LED lights outside monitored space and pulses on low fuel.
- Gyro aim pauses in the main menu, and in the pilot seat if you turn it off there.
- The Home card shows your ship, the star system and your injury.
- Hook points for add-ons: `turn@1` and `state@1`. A language pack add-on patches `data/notifications.json`.
- Needs Bururu with mod API 0.11.
