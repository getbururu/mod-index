# Changelog

Each version has a section, newest first. A release shows its section as its notes.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Haptics for weapons, flight, heat, jumps, docking, landings, shields and hits: 97 feels, each with its own level.
- R2 and L2 follow the situation: hardpoints out, reloading, an empty weapons capacitor, overheating, analysis mode, an interdiction, a hit, the SRV turret, on foot.
- The lightbar shows your hull and the ship's state; the player LEDs show the fire group and the jump countdown; the mic LED shows low fuel and silent running.
- Gyro aim pauses in the main menu and in the panels and maps you pick.
- Reads shields, heat and weapon lists from the HUD.
- Pages in Bururu: Effects (Haptics, Triggers, Lights) and Settings (HUD reader, In menus, Elite's files).
- Hook points for add-ons: `weapon_layer@1`, `turn@1`, `heat@1`, `boost@1` and `state@1`.
- Needs Bururu with mod API 0.10.
