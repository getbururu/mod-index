# Changelog

Each version has a section, newest first. A release shows its section as its notes.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Attack feels from the controller: Swing (a soft swell on each attack) or Bow (a click as the draw starts, a tick at full draw, the string's snap on release), and a light thud when you block.
- R2 follows the attack feel: a light click, or a pull that gets heavier for the bow. L2 resists a little, for the block.
- From Valheim's log: drums when a raid starts, and a slow swell when the night passes in bed (the swell only when you play alone or host).
- The lightbar glows a dim fire colour in a world and blinks red as a raid starts.
- Pages in Bururu: Effects (Haptics, Triggers, Lights) and Settings (Valheim's controls, Valheim's files).
- Hook points for add-ons: `attack@1` and `state@1`.
- Needs Bururu with mod API 0.11.
